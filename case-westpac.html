Westpac

Institutional banking
system design

My role

Lead UX Designer, within a large cross-functional consultancy team.

Overview

As a result of regulatory change, Westpac needed to make significant changes to their off-shore institutional banking systems to bring them on-shore.

With this required change, Westpac also saw an opportunity to consolidate a number of different systems into one, using an existing platform.

This project was to understand the different systems and, working closely with bankers, stakeholders, and the wider Westpac design team, create designs to support the many different user journeys and requirements.

The initial requirement was to rapidly create an MVP to quickly deliver on the regulatory requirement, but at the same time create designs to demonstrate future state capability and test the viability of the intended platform.

Approach

As part of a broader team of business analysts, solution architects and developers, we first conducted a number of workshops to understand and prioritise requirements, and to define approaches.

Then, we worked closely with the product owner, and held further workshops to better understand the more specific requirements for staff at Westpac.

We then created a series of screen designs which were tested with users and the wider business to evaluate business logic, rules, data and functionality. The feedback was then used as input into further iterations.

Solution

The solution is a series of streamlined processes and designs, which have been validated with the staff who will use them every day.

The design enabled the creation of a backlog of user stories for implementation.

Additionally, the design has enabled Westpac to confirm the suitability of their chosen future platform.

The screen designs themselves made use of the existing Westpac design system, allowing efficiencies in our output and allowing for easy alignment with the Westpac design team.

Outcomes
