Case studies

Ryder · United States

Last mile delivery
experience design

My role

Lead UX Designer, overseeing a team comprising a researcher and designer, integrated with a US based client and offshore development team.

Overview

As a long-standing, established player in the North American logistics sector, Ryder was facing profound internal and external challenges in their Last Mile business.

Exponential growth of e-commerce in the past years and resulting increase in volume of deliveries had exposed and exacerbated operational inefficiencies across the business.

Legacy technology inhibited innovation, and an influx of new, nimble competitors was posing a threat to growth and market position.

A rise in expectations from retailers and their consumers around status visibility, customisability, and communication options throughout the delivery process demanded an uplift of the entire B2B2C customer experience.

Approach

Interviewing stakeholders, customers, and users gave us comprehensive insight into current state workflows, and system and task dependencies.

Overlaying this with pain points and needs, we invited users to ideate a future vision for the Last Mile Delivery experience via remote co-design workshops.

From the data and insight gathered throughout the discovery phase, we created a solution concept with a set of potential product features, and helped the client prioritise and define them into an MVP.

The design and implementation of the new platform was rolled out in alignment with the enterprise level strategic transformation roadmap.

The design and development teams embedded with Ryder's in-house technology group and delivered the product in agile fashion over the course of two release cycles.

Solution

We created an intuitive, unified experience for Ryder Last Mile staff, customers, and their end consumers, that integrated seamlessly with existing off-the-shelf solutions.

We focused on streamlining the IA and related task flows, minimising screen volume and nesting, and reducing cognitive load on users by radically simplifying layouts and interactions.

The creation of a library of UI and interaction patterns ensures future-proof consistency in the experience throughout coming releases.
